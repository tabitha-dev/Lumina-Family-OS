About Lumina Family OS

What can I organize here?

Plan routines, chores, family events, meal ideas, shopping lists, wellness habits and star rewards in one browser-based home dashboard.

Is Lumina free, and where is my data saved?

Lumina is free and needs no sign-in. Changes are stored in this browser on this device. They do not automatically sync between devices. Clear browser site data to remove them.

Does Lumina access my location?

No automatic location request is needed. Set a city in Settings to look up current weather with Open-Meteo. Recipe searches are sent to TheMealDB.
